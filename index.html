Kenghou Hoi

I am a master's student in Control Science and Engineering at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Fei Gao at FAST Lab. My research focuses on aerial manipulation, UAV motion planning and control, and reinforcement learning. I received my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications. I am from Macao SAR, China.

Research Interests

My research interests include aerial manipulation, tactile sensing, robot perception and control, motion planning, reinforcement learning, and autonomous aerial systems. I am particularly interested in robust onboard systems that combine visual and force feedback for agile, contact-rich interaction in real-world environments.
