Kenghou Hoi

I am a master's student in Control Science and Engineering at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Fei Gao at FAST Lab. My research focuses on manipulation, tactile sensing, robot perception and control, motion planning, and reinforcement learning. I received my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications. I am from Macao SAR, China.

Research Interests

My research interests include manipulation, tactile sensing, robot perception and control, motion planning, reinforcement learning, and autonomous robotic systems. I am particularly interested in robust systems that combine visual and force feedback for contact-rich interaction in real-world environments.
